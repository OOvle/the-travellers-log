**2 Actions, 1 Energy**

Choose a target within 12 yuels of yourself that you can see. **The target must make an END resistance roll (DR 7 + WIS)**. On a failed roll, the target bursts into flames and suffers **2d6 fire damage ([[2. Core Rules#Damage Properties|surefire]])**. At the start of each of its turns, the burning target suffers **1d6 fire damage ([[2. Core Rules#Damage Properties|surefire]])** until the fire is put out with 1 action or the target submerges into a safe liquid. On a successful roll, they only suffer half of the rolled damage.
