**2 Actions, 2 Energy**

Choose a target within 12 yuels of yourself. The chosen target suffers **2d8 electric damage ([[2. Core Rules#Damage Properties|unavoidable]])**. You may choose up to 2 additional targets within 6 yuels of your original target. Each of the additionally chosen targets suffer **half of the rolled damage ([[2. Core Rules#Damage Properties|unavoidable]])**.
