**2 Actions, 5 Energy**

Choose a target within 100 yuels of yourself. That target suffers **1d12 arcane damage, 1d10 electric damage, 1d8 fire damage, 1d6 freezing damage, and 1d4 poison damage**. **All other characters within 6 yuels of the target suffer the rolled fire damage ([[2. Core Rules#Damage Properties|unavoidable]])**. You may choose up to 3 additional targets within 12 yuels of your original target, **they suffer the same amount of electric damage the original target suffered ([[2. Core Rules#Damage Properties|unavoidable]])**. **The original target must make a MGT resistance roll (DR 7 + WIS)**. On a failed roll, the target becomes **dazed** and **slowed** until the end of the next round.
