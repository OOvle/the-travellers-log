**2 Actions, 3 Energy**

Create a wall that is up to 8 yuels long within 12 yuels of yourself. The wall is 1 yuel thick and 4 yuels tall. You may make the wall take any shape along the ground that you wish, but the wall cannot exceed 8 yuels in length. When the wall is first created and when any character who ends their turn within 1 yuel of this wall suffers **1d12 freezing damage ([[2. Core Rules#Damage Properties|surefire]])**. The wall is considered unstable terrain when climbing or moving on top of it. Each segment of the wall has 30 health and has an **Innate Normal defense of 4**.
