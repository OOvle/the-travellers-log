As player characters advance in level, they gain access to an increasing number of abilities that they may choose from. Many skills will call for you to make an attack roll between melee, ranged, or magic. The weapon you are using must have the matching tag to use the skill in question.

>**Example**: you cannot perform _taunting strike_ with a bow (ranged), but you can perform _volley_ with a bow.

**All abilities may be acquired once each.**
