All characters have a category and size. Below is the size chart, which breaks down how big they would appear on the map.

|   Size   | Area |  Spaces   |
| :------: | :--: | :-------: |
|   Tiny   | ¼^2  |  ¼ space  |
|  Small   | 1^2  |  1 space  |
|  Medium  | 1^2  |  1 space  |
|  Large   | 2^2  | 4 spaces  |
|   Huge   | 3^2  | 9 spaces  |
| Massive  | 4^2  | 16 spaces |
| Colossal | 5^2  | 25 spaces |
## Categories
There are the following categories for characters: **Abomination, Beast, Floral, Humanoid, Insect, Nightmare, Puppet, Reverent, Spirit, Undead, and Behemoth**.
