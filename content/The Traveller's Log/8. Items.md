# Tags
Here is a list of general tags you will see within each category of items and a description.
##### Armor 
- **Light.** This armor applies your **AGI** to dodge rolls.
- **Medium.** This armor grants you a bonus to block rolls.
- **Heavy.** This armor grants you a bonus **innate defense** and block rolls at the expense of your dodge rolls.

##### Weapons
- **Armor Piercing.** This weapon ignores [[2. Core Rules#Innate Defense|innate defense]].
- **Blunt.** The weapon bludgeons the target to do damage.
- **Concealable.** This weapon may be hidden easily. Gain [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|favor]] on rolls to conceal this weapon from search attempts.
- **Free-Hand.** This weapon does not require you to hold it in either hand. It still takes up your main hand slot, but you may use your main hand as if you were not holding anything in it.
- **Heavy.** Requires the user to have MGT +1 or higher. If this is not met, attacks made with this weapon suffer [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|disfavor]]. Throw range is equal to half of your MGT (minimum 1 yuel).
- **Melee.** The item has a melee range of 1 yuel.
- **Parrying.** This weapon grants you +1 to block rolls (does not stack with itself).
- **Profane**. This weapon calls upon the unholy to bring harm.
- **Ranged**. This item uses ammunition to attack from a distance. Without the required ammo, this weapon may not perform attack rolls.
- **Reach.** The weapon has a melee range of 2 yuels.
- **Sacred**. This weapon calls upon the holy to pass judgement.
- **Siege.** This weapon deals double damage to structures and objects.
- **Slash**. The weapon slashes the target to do damage.
- **Stab.** The weapon punctures the target to do damage.
- **Thrown.** This weapon may be thrown effectively, up to 8+MGT yuels. Weapons that do not possess this property have a thrown range equal to your MGT instead (minimum 1 yuel).
- **2-Handed**. Requires two hands to wield.
- **Versatile.** This weapon may be used as a one-handed weapon or a two-handed weapon. Deals bonus damage when wielded with two-hands.
- **Weighted.** Characters that takes any damage from attacks with this weapon are pushed 1 yuel away from you. Throw range is equal to half of your MGT (minimum 1 yuel).

##### Consumables
- **Ammunition**. A consumable resource for ranged weapons, such as the [[Bow]].
- **Restorative**. Restores health, energy, or other.
