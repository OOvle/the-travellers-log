These are a major point of your character’s strength. Every adventurer has one and it shapes how they handle self-defense. Whether you are skilled with weaponry, seek arcane knowledge, or carry divine blessings, you will have something that sets you apart from the others. Though these are remarkably strong and bring a lot to the table, they are not the final deciding factor of how you’re supposed to play! Get creative, mix and match these in ways that are unprecedented!

You’ll notice that each major trait has multiple ranks. All of them show a “(Level x).” If you want to acquire the effects of the next rank for your character, you must choose the “Rank Up Major Trait” at the matching level of your chosen major trait. For example: If you want to rank up Artisan from rank 1 to rank 2, you must be level 4 to choose this option.

Of course, you aren’t required to maximize on these; you don’t have to upgrade them when you reach the required level for the next rank.
# [[Artisan]]
You are a skilled craftsman. You spend your days mastering your craft, always looking for ways to make the process more efficient. You aim to craft your magnum opus one day.

**Rank 1:**
- All crafting may be done at half of the required time.

**Rank 2 (Level 4):**
- All crafting may be done at half of the required resources.

**Rank 3 (Level 9):**
- When salvaging, you get to choose two materials you get instead of rolling for one. 
- Your craft precedes you. You can sell items that you craft for double their usual value.
- Anyone who sees your craft will recognize your craftsmanship.
# [[Fighter]]
You are remarkable at melee combat. Your prowess with weaponry is above most. When you are on the battlefield, your enemies tremble as you are a force to reckon with.

**Rank 1:**
- Your blunt, slash, stab, ranged, and unarmed weapon mastery ranks are now 2.

**Rank** **2** **(Level 5):**
- When performing a weapon attack, add your **prowess level** to the damage total. 
- Your blunt, slash, stab, ranged, and unarmed weapon mastery ranks that are not already 3 becomes rank 3.

**Rank 3 (level 10):**
- You cannot suffer disfavor with weapon attacks. 
- You may **expend 2 energy** during your turn to grant yourself 2 additional actions for your current turn. You may not do this more than once per round.
# [[Guardian]]
Your defense is unbreakable. When you take a stand, you are an immovable wall that your opponents simply cannot move past. If anyone if going to break the line of defense, they must get through you first.

**Rank 1:**
- Your blunt weapon mastery becomes 2. 
- If you take the defend action, you automatically win contests to resist being **wrestled**.
- While you’re defending, you may choose to redirect an attack to you if you are within range of the attack being performed.

**Rank 2 (Level 3):**
- Increase your block dice size by 1.
- If you take the defend action, roll **1pd. All friendly characters within 6 yuels of yourself (including yourself)** add your roll to their block rolls until the end of this round.

**Rank 3 (Level 10):**
- Increase your block dice size by 1.
- You cannot suffer disfavor with block rolls and you can block attacks that normally cannot be blocked by **expending 1 energy**.
# [[Late Bloomer]]
You’re nothing special… For now.

**Rank 1:**
- Instead of either the standard array or point buy, you must start with the following stat array: 
	- 0, 0, 0, -1, -1.
- All of your stats may be increased to a maximum of +7 instead of +6.

**Rank 2 (Level 6):**
- Increase all of your stats by +1, up to a maximum of +7.

**Rank 3 (Level 12):**
- Increase all of your stats by +2, up to a maximum of +7.
- You now have **prowess level 7 and a d20 prowess die**.
- When rolling disfavor, the roll becomes 1d10 instead of your **prowess die**.
# [[Magus]]
Skilled in the magical arts, you hone your mind continuously. Controlling the elements as if they were part of your own body.

**Rank 1:**
- Increase your max energy by 2.
- Your magic weapon mastery is now level 2.
- When you deal **Arcane, Electric, Fire, Freezing, or Poison damage**, add your **prowess level** to the damage total as **arcane damage**. Regardless of how many listed damage types are roll from this list, you add your **prowess level** only once to the final damage calculation.

**Rank 2 (Level 3):**
- Increase your **Elemental Innate Defense** by 1.
- Reduce the energy cost of abilities that deal **Arcane, Electric, Fire, Freezing, or Poison** **damage** by 1 (to a minimum of 1).

**Rank 3 (Level 10):** You may **expend up to 4 additional energy** when you use an ability that would deal **Arcane, Electric, Fire, Freezing, or Poison damage**. If you do this, you may choose a number of effects equal to the energy spent from the following (applying each effect a maximum of once each):
- **Splash:** Instead of a target character, choose a target location within range of the ability. **All characters within a 3 yuel radius at the chosen location** are hit by the ability.
- **Morph:** Change the damage type of the ability to be one of the following: **Physical, Arcane, Electric, Fire, Freezing, or Poison damage**.
- **Pierce:** Damage from this ability ignores **Innate Defense and Damage Resistance**.
- **Potent:** Increase the dice size of the damage roll(s) by 1 (maximum of d12).

# [[Marksman]]
You are precise with ranged weaponry. Seemingly never missing your mark, you always find a way to hit even the most “impossible” of shots.

**Rank 1:**
- Your weapon mastery with ranged weapons is now 2.
- When using ranged weapons, you have double the effective range.

**Rank 2 (Level 5):**
- Anything less than total cover does not impose disfavor to your ranged weapon attack rolls.
- Whenever you make a ranged weapon attack to hit a single target (including abilities), you may **expend 1 energy** to target an additional character within range using the same damage roll. You may target up to a number of additional targets equal to your **AGI** (expending energy to match the number of additional targets you wish to hit; minimum of 1).

**Rank 3 (Level 10):**
- Your accuracy with ranged weaponry is refined to an impossible degree. You cannot suffer disfavor with ranged weapon attacks.
- When you make a ranged weapon attack, you ignore **Innate Defense and Damage Resistance**.
# [[Radiant]]
Your piety is seen across the lands. You carry divine blessing with every step, and people recognize your sanctity.

**Rank 1:**
- You have resistance to **Dark and Mental damage**. 
- When rolling to restore life to a character with an ability, add **1pd** to the amount restored.

**Rank 2 (Level 6):**
- You have resistance to **Soul damage**.
- You cannot be **terrified** or **charmed**.
- When rolling for **Holy damage**, increase it by your **prowess level**.

**Rank 3 (Level 10):**
- You cannot suffer disfavor when making resistance rolls. 
- Nightmares suffer disfavor on all rolls against you.
- **All friendly characters within 12 yuels of yourself** gain resistance to **Dark, Mental, and Soul damage**.
- **All friendly characters within 6 yuels of yourself** are immune to the effects of **terrified and charmed**, but are not cured of them if already under the effects.
# [[Tamer]]
You have a tamed creature that follows your orders and helps you in battle.

Gain a tamed creature as a companion. You may choose any small or medium sized **Beast, Floral, or Insect** creature. If you would like a large sized companion, you may discuss with your GM what it would be, but the large companion would not gain any mechanical benefits.
- When you level up, you may forego one of your two level up options to gain a new ability for your companion. The options you may select from are based on which type of companion you have (beast, floral, or insect). 
    - **TESTING:** You may use both of your level up options to power up your companion if you so wish.
- Once per long rest, you may **expend 2 energy** to revive your companion if it has perished.
## Tamer Companion Character Sheet
When you take the tamer major trait, you will also acquire a companion character sheet. This will be used to help keep track of what your companion is able to do along side your choices you make when leveling up your companion.
### Beast
A beast has the following stats:

| Max Health: 10 | Max Energy: 5 | Block Roll: 1d4 | Dodge Roll: 1d10 | Move Speed: 7 |
| -------------- | ------------- | --------------- | ---------------- | ------------- |
| AGI 2          | MGT 2         | END 0           | WLL -1           | WIS -1        |
- When a beast levels up, its max health increases by 3.
- Melee attack range of 1 yuel.
- If the beast you choose has wings, it may hover, but it cannot fly.

### Floral
A floral has the following stats:

| Max Health: 8 | Max Energy: 7 | Block Roll: 1d4 | Dodge Roll: 1d8 | Move Speed: 6 |
| ------------- | ------------- | --------------- | --------------- | ------------- |
| AGI 0         | MGT 0         | END -2          | WLL 2           | WIS 2         |
- When a floral levels up, its max health increases by 2.
- Melee attack range of 4 yuels.
### Insect
An insect has the following stats:

| Max Health: 12 | Max Energy: 4 | Block Roll: 1d6 | Dodge Roll: 1d6 | Move Speed: 6 |
| -------------- | ------------- | --------------- | --------------- | ------------- |
| AGI 1          | MGT 1         | END 2           | WLL -1          | WIS -1        |
- When an insect levels up, its max health increases by 4.
- Melee attack range of 1 yuel.
- If the insect you choose has wings, it may hover, but it cannot fly.
### All Companions
The abilities that the companion may choose can be found on page 61. The damage roll of your companion’s attack is based on your **prowess dice**.
