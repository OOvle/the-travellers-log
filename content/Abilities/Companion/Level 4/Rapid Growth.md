##### **Companion Type**: Floral

**2 Actions, 2 Energy, Up to 1 minute**

Your companion becomes **[[Rooted|rooted]]**. Cover the floor in wild growth riddled with thorns in a 6 yuel radius around your companion. The growth is considered difficult terrain. **All characters who move through it for the first time each turn must make a MGT resistance roll (DR 8 + prowess level)**. On a failed roll, that character suffers **1pd poison damage ([[2. Core Rules#Damage Properties|surefire]])** and **1pd physical damage ([[2. Core Rules#Damage Properties|surefire]])** and loses the rest of their movement for that turn. On a successful roll, they only suffer half of the rolled physical and poison damage.
