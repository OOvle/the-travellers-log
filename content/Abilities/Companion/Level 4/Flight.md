##### **Companion Type**: Any
**Passive**

Your companion gains a flying speed equal to its move speed. It may carry a number of characters and still fly at normal speed equal to half of its **MGT**. It may only move at half of its flying speed if it exceeds its carry limit by 1. If it exceeds its carry limit by 2 or more, it cannot fly.
