##### **Companion Type**: Any
**Passive**

When your companion uses an action that uses your [[3. Character Creation#Prowess|prowess level]], it’s treated as if you had [[3. Character Creation#Prowess|prowess level]] 7 and [[3. Character Creation#Prowess|prowess dice]] rolls become d20’s. Additionally, your companion may use your energy as though it were its own.
