##### **Companion Type**: Beast

**Passive**

Your companion becomes an alpha beast. **All other friendly characters within 6 yuels of this companion** deals an additional **1d6 soul damage ([[2. Core Rules#Damage Properties|unblockable]])** when rolling for damage.

**2 Actions, 3 Energy, 4 hours**

You gain control of **2 temporary additional lesser companions**, which may only perform 1 action during your companion’s turn (these may be actions that cost either 1 or 2 actions, but once performed, their turn ends). Each of these companions have half of your companion’s stats (**AGI, MGT, END, WLL, WIS**, max health, and max energy). Their move speed is equal to 6 yuels. They have access to abilities and passives from level 4 and lower that your companion has.
