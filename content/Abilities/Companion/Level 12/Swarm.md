##### **Companion Type**: Insect

**2 Actions, 3 Energy, 10 minutes**

Bring forth a swarm of insects covering a 3 yuel radius. You may move this swarm up to 8 yuels during each of your companion's turns. Hostile characters caught inside this swarm suffer [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|disfavor]] to damage rolls and suffer **1d8 physical damage ([[2. Core Rules#Damage Properties|surefire]])** if they end their turn within the swarm. All friendly characters within the swarm gain [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|favor]] when rolling to block melee attacks and when rolling to dodge ranged attacks.
