##### **Companion Type**: Insect

**2 Actions, 1 Energy**, 1 minute

Your companion chooses a target within 4 yuels of itself. Whenever that target is about to receive damage, the damage roll suffers [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|disfavor]]. This lasts for up to 1 minute, or until a new target is chosen with this ability.
