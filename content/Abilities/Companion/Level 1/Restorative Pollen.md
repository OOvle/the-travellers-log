##### **Companion Type**: Floral

**2 Actions, 2 Energy**

Spread a cloud of pollen that restores the health of all characters within 3 yuels of itself by **1pd**. It restores health again at the start of the next round for **half of the rolled amount** to any characters that are in the cloud. The cloud does not move with your companion.
