##### **Companion Type**: Floral

**Reaction, 1 Energy**

When your companion performs an attack roll, it may attempt to restrict its target as part of the attack. If the target does not successfully dodge the attack, **they must make an AGI resistance roll (DR 8 + prowess level)**. On a failed roll, the target and your companion becomes **[[Rooted|rooted]]** until they make a wrestle contest to break free or your companion releases them. Your companion gains [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|favor]] to wrestle contests against its **[[Rooted|rooted]]** target during this time.
