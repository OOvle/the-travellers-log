##### **Companion Type**: Floral

**2 Actions, 3 Energy, Focus (up to 1 minute)**

Your companion attunes to the sound of nature. While focusing on this ability:

- Increase your companion's attack range by 5 yuels (attacks made by this companion are still considered melee).
- When your companion makes a melee attack roll, increase the damage by **1pd poison damage**.
- Anytime your companion rolls to restore health, that roll gains [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|favor]].
- Once per turn, your companion may teleport to a location that is natural terrain within 12 yuels of itself (Such as: soil, rocks, shrubs, and so on).
