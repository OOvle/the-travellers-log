##### **Companion Type**: Beast

**1 Action, 2 Energy, Focus (up to 1 minute)**

Your companion enrages. While enraged, it deals an additional **1pd physical damage** to all damage rolls, becomes **hasted** for the duration, and suffers [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|disfavor]] to block and dodge rolls.
