**Reaction, 1 Energy**

When you perform a ranged weapon attack, enchant your ammo. The attack deals an additional **1pd poison damage**. If the target does not successfully dodge the attack, **they must make an AGI resistance roll (DR 7 + AGI)**. On a failed roll, the target becomes **[[Rooted|rooted]]** until the end of the next round.
