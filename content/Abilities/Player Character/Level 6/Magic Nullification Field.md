**2 Actions, 2 Energy, Focus (up to 10 minutes)**

Choose a target location within 12 yuels of yourself. Create a visible magical field that is 5 yuels in diameter that nullifies all **arcane, elemental, holy, dark, and mental damage types** centered at the target location. Any projectiles that would pass through the field that matches the listed damage types simply stops at the edge of the field, fizzling out. Any area effects with the listed damage types do not expand into the magical field. Physical projectiles imbued with the listed damage types lose their properties and only maintain the **physical damage** within the field. Melee attacks imbued with the listed damage types only deal **physical damage** within the field. Any magical effects (such as [[Mystical Weave]] or [[Guardian Angel]]) lose their effect within the field, but do not get interrupted.
