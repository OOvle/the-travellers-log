**Passive**

When you perform a melee weapon attack, you may choose to make it with reckless abandon. If you do so, you gain [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|favor]] for every attack roll that you make until the end of this round. All attacks against you gain [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|favor]] until the end of the next round.
