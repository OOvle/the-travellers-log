**1 Action, 1 Energy**

You may only use this action if you’ve moved at least 3 yuels in a mostly straight line. Perform a melee weapon attack. For every yuel you traveled before using this ability, increase the damage of the attack roll by +1.

>Note: The GM determines if the path traveled is straight enough to perform this action and the total bonus damage for the roll.
