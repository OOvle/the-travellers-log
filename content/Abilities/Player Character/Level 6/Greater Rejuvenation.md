**2 Actions (1 Action), 3 Energy, Focus (up to 1 minute)**

When you use this ability, all friendly characters within 12 yuels of yourself (including yourself) restore 1d6+WLL health. Once per each subsequent turn, you may use **1 action** to roll the restored health again.
