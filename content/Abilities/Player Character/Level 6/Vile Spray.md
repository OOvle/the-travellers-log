**2 Actions, 2 Energy, 1 minute**

Choose a target within 4 yuels of yourself. You unleash a splash a vile poison on the target. All characters within 1 yuel of the target suffers **2d8 poison damage ([[2. Core Rules#Damage Properties|unblockable]])**. If the target does not successfully dodge the attack, **they must make an END resistance roll (DR 7 + WIS)**. Each character that fails the roll becomes **poisoned**. At the start of each round, affected characters suffer **1d4 poison damage ([[2. Core Rules#Damage Properties|surefire]])**, and suffers [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|disfavor]] to attack rolls. This status persists up to 1 minute.
