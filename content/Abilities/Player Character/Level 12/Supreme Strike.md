**2 Actions, 4 Energy**

Perform a melee weapon attack that is **[[2. Core Rules#Damage Properties|unavoidable]]**. The damage of this attack is increased by **1pd as physical damage**. If the target becomes **[[2. Core Rules#Health And Energy|wounded]]** from this attack, the target becomes **stunned** until the end of the next round and takes an additional **1pd as mental damage ([[2. Core Rules#Damage Properties|surefire]])**.
