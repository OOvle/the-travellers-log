**2 Actions, 4 Energy, Focus (up to 1 minute)**

Roll **2d12+WLL**. All friendly characters within 50 yuels that you can see (including yourself) **restore life** equal to the amount rolled. All hostile characters that you can see suffer the rolled amount as **holy damage.**
Choose a willing character that you can see. The chosen target becomes favored by the creator. For the next 1 minute, the target cannot suffer [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|disfavor]] to any rolls and cannot be effected any status effect that they do not willfully accept.
