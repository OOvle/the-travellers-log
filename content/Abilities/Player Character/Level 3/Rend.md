**Reaction, 1 Energy**

When you perform a **slash or stab** melee weapon attack, empower your strike. If the target does not successfully dodge the attack, **they must make an END resistance roll (DR 7 + MGT)**. On a failed roll, the target becomes **[[2. Core Rules#Damage Modifiers|vulnerable]] to poison damage** until the end of the next round, and at the start of the next round, the target loses **1d8** health from blood loss (this does not affect puppets, reverents, or spirits).
