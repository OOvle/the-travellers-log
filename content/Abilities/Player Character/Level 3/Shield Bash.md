**2 Actions, 1 Energy**

Perform a melee weapon attack with your shield, using your block roll as the damage for this attack. If the target does not successfully dodge the attack, **they must make a MGT resistance roll (DR 7 + MGT)**. On a failed roll, the target becomes **dazed** until the end of the next round.
