**Reaction, 2 Energy**

If a character within melee range of you attempts to perform an action, **you may roll a MGT skill check (DR 8 plus the prowess level of your target)**. If you succeed the [[2. Core Rules#Skill checks|skill check]], the target fails their action, but keeps any energy or other resources required to use the ability.
