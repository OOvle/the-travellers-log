**2 Actions, 2 Energy, 10 minutes**

Choose a target location within 24 yuels of yourself. Create a cloud of poison that has a radius of 3 yuels around the target location that lasts for up to 10 minutes or until dispersed by a powerful wind. All characters who are inside the mist when it’s created or at the end of their turn suffers **1d8 poison damage ([[2. Core Rules#Damage Properties|surefire]])**. Whenever a character suffers poison damage from this ability, **they must make an END resistance roll (DR 7 + WIS)**. On a failed roll, they become **[[Dazed]]** until the end of the next round.
