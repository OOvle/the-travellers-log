**2 Actions, 2 Energy, Focus (up to 1 minute)**

Choose a willing character within 6 yuels of yourself. Begin focusing to cause the target to become protected. While a character is protected by this ability, they gain [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|favor]] on all resistance rolls and add your **WLL** to their block and dodge rolls (minimum 1).
