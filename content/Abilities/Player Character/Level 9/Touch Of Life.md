**2 Actions, 2 Energy**

Touch a character. Your target is restored to full health and gains a protective light that blocks the next source of incoming damage completely once. This effect triggers automatically upon receiving any damage. The protective light lasts until it blocks damage, your next long rest, or until you use this skill on another character. Protective light cannot stack with itself.
