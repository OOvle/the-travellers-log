**Reaction, 1 Energy**

When you perform a **blunt** melee weapon attack, empower your strike. You can put an intense amount of force into the attack, increasing the damage by **1d10 physical damage**. If the target does not successfully dodge the attack, **they must make a MGT resistance roll (DR 7 + MGT).** On a failed roll, the target is pushed up to 5 yuels in any lateral direction you wish.
