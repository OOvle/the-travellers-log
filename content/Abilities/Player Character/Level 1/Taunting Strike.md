**Reaction, 1 Energy**

When you perform a melee weapon attack, empower your strike. Increase the damage of this attack by **1d6 mental damage ([[2. Core Rules#Damage Properties|unavoidable]])**. **The target must make a WLL resistance roll (DR 7 + MGT).** On a failed roll, the target becomes **taunted** until the end of this round.
