**2 Actions, 1 Energy**

Choose a target within 12 yuels of yourself that you can see. **The target must make an AGI resistance roll (DR 7 + WIS)**. On a failed roll, the target suffers **1d8 poison damage ([[2. Core Rules#Damage Properties|surefire]])** and becomes **[[Rooted|rooted]]** until the end of the next round.
