**2 Actions, 1 Energy**

Choose a direction. **All other characters within 3 yuels of yourself must make a MGT resistance roll (DR 7 + WIS)**. Each character that fails the roll is pushed up to 3 yuels in the chosen direction and suffers **2d4 freezing damage ([[2. Core Rules#Damage Properties|surefire]])**. Each character that succeeds the resistance roll only suffers half of the rolled damage.
