All information in regards to your character are tracked on a _character sheet_. Character sheets are used for keeping track of your health, energy, defensive stats, and much more. In this chapter, we’ll go over each part of the player character and what each component does as we create a character together with Jade. Our imaginary friend Jade will hopefully help you understand the process and choices you make as you create your brand new character!

# Character Stats

**Stats** are broken into 5 total:

- **Agility (AGI)** represents your balance, precision and reflexes.
- **Might (MGT)** represents your physical prowess.
- **Endurance (END)** represents your ability to persevere, withstand intense weather, and resist poisons.
- **Will (WLL)** represents your social presence, determination, and discipline.
- **Wisdom (WIS)** represents your observation and reasoning.

A new, level 1 character has a stat spread of +2, +1, 0, 0, -1. Apply each number from this array to one of your five stats – Agility (**AGI**), Might (**MGT**), Endurance (**END**), Will (**WLL**), and Wisdom (**WIS**) – **assigning each number exactly once.**

Jade decides to sort the stats as follows: **AGI +2, MGT 0, END 0, WLL +1, and WIS -1**. She chooses this spread as she wants her character to be good with his hands, but a little bit head strong too.

## Optional Point Buy

Alternatively, you can do point buy and assign points how you want. With all 5 stats being baseline 0, you have 2 points to allocate into them freely. You may choose to reduce a stat by -1 to gain +1 point to spend in a different stat. You may not raise any one stat beyond 3 and you may not lower any one stat below -2. The sum of your stats should always equate to +2.

Using this optional rule, Jade decides she wants to take two points away from **WIS** and allocate 2 points into **AGI** and the last two into **MGT & WLL**, leaving Gimbli at AGI **+2, MGT +1, END 0, WLL +1, and WIS -2**. This stat array is legal because when all numbers are added together, it becomes +2.

> **Note:** This is explained only for the example, point buy will not be used when calculating Gimbli’s stats.

# Define Your Stats

Your **max health** is equal to **9, plus your current level (typically 1 for new characters), plus your END**. A negative **END** stat will reduce your starting max health.

Your **max energy** is equal to **5 plus your WIS**. A negative **WIS** stat will reduce your starting max energy.

Your **block roll** is equal to **1d4**. Your armor, shield, bloodline, and major trait may give you bonuses to this.

Your **dodge roll** is equal to **1d8**. Your armor and bloodline may give you bonuses to this.

Your **move speed** is equal to **6**.

Jade’s character, Gimbli, has the following stats: 10 max health, 4 max energy, block roll of 1d6 (Duroka bloodline increases it: 1d4 → 1d6), dodge roll of 1d8, and finally a move speed of 6.

Prowess affects many parts of your character. It improves your favor and disfavor dice size, it improves the damage of your abilities, and much more!

# Starting Equipment

Each character has the following equipment slots: Main hand, off-hand, armor, and three accessories. **Your main hand** is your dominant hand. The one you would typically wield your weapon in. You may equip a two-handed weapon, but that will also take up your off-hand slot in addition to your main hand slot. **Your off-hand** can be used to wield a weapon or shield. You may only have **one armor equipped** at any given time. You may wear **a maximum of 3 accessories** at any given time.

# Origin & Motive

Finally, define your origin and motive. Your character’s origin can be just about anything from being a soldier, a bandit, a pirate, a nobleman or noblewoman, to even a commoner who simply sought out adventure. Your character’s motive is the driving force of all their actions and choices. Using a short sentence or single-word emotion, create a motive to your character’s actions. Some simple examples of character motives: Adventure, Justice, Duty sworn, Regaining family honor, Guilt, or Pride. This can change during the adventure to match the character’s growth. This gives you some role playing elements to your character that you can use to shape your character’s actions.

Jade writes down that Gimbli grew up in the workshop, spending his days crafting and making jewelry. His motive is to find rare metals and jewels to create truly astounding works of craftsmanship.

# Level Up Options

When a character levels up, they get to pick two options from the following list:

1. **Increase Max Health**: Your Max Health is increased by **1pd** or you END, **whenever** is higher.
2. **Increase Max Energy**: Your Max Energy is increased by 1.
3. **Gain New Ability**: Choose an ability that you do not already have and add it to your list of abilities. You may only choose abilities from your level or lower.
4. **Improve Major Trait**: Your Major Trait ranks up to the next rank. You must be the listed level to take this option.
5. **Improve Stat**: Increase one stat by +1 (up to a maximum of +6). You may only choose this option once per level.
6. **Improve Mastery**: Improve one of your masteries (up to d12).
