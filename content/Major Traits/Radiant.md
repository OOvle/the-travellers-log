Your piety is seen across the lands. You carry divine blessing with every step, and people recognize your sanctity.

**Rank 1:**

- You have [[2. Core Rules#Damage Modifiers|resistance]] to **Dark and Mental damage**.
- When rolling to restore life to a character with an ability, add **1pd** to the amount restored.

**Rank 2 (Level 6):**

- You have [[2. Core Rules#Damage Modifiers|resistance]] to **Soul damage**.
- You cannot be **terrified** or **charmed**.
- When rolling for **Holy damage**, increase it by your **[[3. Character Creation#Prowess|prowess level]]**.

**Rank 3 (Level 10):**

- You cannot suffer [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|disfavor]] when making resistance rolls.
- Nightmares suffer [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|disfavor]] on all rolls against you.
- **All friendly characters within 12 yuels of yourself** gain [[2. Core Rules#Damage Modifiers|resistance]] to **Dark, Mental, and Soul damage**.
- **All friendly characters within 6 yuels of yourself** are immune to the effects of **terrified and charmed**, but are not cured of them if already under the effects.
