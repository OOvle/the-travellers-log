You are a skilled craftsman. You spend your days mastering your craft, always looking for ways to make the process more efficient. You aim to craft your magnum opus one day.

**Rank 1:**

- All crafting may be done at half of the required time.

**Rank 2 (Level 4):**

- All crafting may be done at half of the required resources.

**Rank 3 (Level 9):**

- When salvaging, you get to choose two materials you get instead of rolling for one.
- Your craft precedes you. You can sell items that you craft for double their usual value.
- Anyone who sees your craft will recognize your craftsmanship.
