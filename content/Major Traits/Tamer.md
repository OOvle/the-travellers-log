You have a tamed creature that follows your orders and helps you in battle.

Gain a tamed creature as a companion. You may choose any small or medium sized **Beast, Floral, or Insect** creature. If you would like a large sized companion, you may discuss with your GM what it would be, but the large companion would not gain any mechanical benefits.

- When you level up, you may forego one of your two level up options to gain a new ability for your companion. The options you may select from are based on which type of companion you have (beast, floral, or insect).
  - **TESTING:** You may use both of your level up options to power up your companion if you so wish.
- Once per [[2. Core Rules#Resting|long rest]], you may **expend 2 energy** to revive your companion if it has perished.

# Tamer Companion Character Sheet

When you take the tamer major trait, you will also acquire a companion character sheet. This will be used to help keep track of what your companion is able to do along side your choices you make when leveling up your companion.

## Beast

A beast has the following stats:

| Max Health: 10 | Max Energy: 5 | Block Roll: 1d4 | Dodge Roll: 1d10 | Move Speed: 7 |
| -------------- | ------------- | --------------- | ---------------- | ------------- |
| AGI 2          | MGT 2         | END 0           | WLL -1           | WIS -1        |

- When a beast levels up, its max health increases by 3.
- Melee attack range of 1 yuel.
- If the beast you choose has wings, it may hover, but it cannot fly.

## Floral

A floral has the following stats:

| Max Health: 8 | Max Energy: 7 | Block Roll: 1d4 | Dodge Roll: 1d8 | Move Speed: 6 |
| ------------- | ------------- | --------------- | --------------- | ------------- |
| AGI 0         | MGT 0         | END -2          | WLL 2           | WIS 2         |

- When a floral levels up, its max health increases by 2.
- Melee attack range of 4 yuels.

## Insect

An insect has the following stats:

| Max Health: 12 | Max Energy: 4 | Block Roll: 1d6 | Dodge Roll: 1d6 | Move Speed: 6 |
| -------------- | ------------- | --------------- | --------------- | ------------- |
| AGI 1          | MGT 1         | END 2           | WLL -1          | WIS -1        |

- When an insect levels up, its max health increases by 4.
- Melee attack range of 1 yuel.
- If the insect you choose has wings, it may hover, but it cannot fly.

## All Companions

The abilities that the companion may choose can be found on page 61. The damage roll of your companion’s attack is based on your **prowess dice**.
