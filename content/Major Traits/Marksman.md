You are precise with ranged weaponry. Seemingly never missing your mark, you always find a way to hit even the most “impossible” of shots.

**Rank 1:**
- Your [[2. Core Rules#Weapon Mastery|weapon mastery]] with ranged weapons is now 2.
- When using ranged weapons, you have double the effective range.

**Rank 2 (Level 5):**
- Anything less than total cover does not impose [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|disfavor]] to your ranged weapon attack rolls.
- Whenever you make a ranged weapon attack to hit a single target (including abilities), you may **expend 1 energy** to target an additional character within range using the same damage roll. You may target a number of additional targets equal to your **AGI** (expending energy to match the number of additional targets you wish to hit; minimum of 1).

**Rank 3 (Level 10):**
- Your accuracy with ranged weaponry is refined to an impossible degree. You cannot suffer [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|disfavor]] with ranged weapon attacks.
- When you make a ranged weapon attack, you ignore **[[2. Core Rules#Innate Defense|Innate Defense]] and [[2. Core Rules#Damage Modifiers|Damage Resistance]]**.
