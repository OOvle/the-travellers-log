Skilled in the magical arts, you hone your mind continuously. Controlling the elements as if they were part of your own body.

> "Spell Damage" is a term that is used to refer to the following damage types: **Arcane, Electric, Fire, Freezing, and Poison.**

**Rank 1:**
- Increase your max energy by 2.
- Your magic [[2. Core Rules#Weapon Mastery|weapon mastery]] is now level 2.
- When you deal **Spell Damage**, add your **prowess level** to the damage total as **arcane damage**. Regardless of how many listed damage types are rolled from this list, you add your **prowess level** only once to the final damage calculation.
- When you would deal **Spell Damage**, you may **expend 1 additional energy** to manipluate the spell you are about to cast: Instead of a target character, choose a target location within range of the ability. **All characters within a 3 yuel radius at the chosen location** are hit by the ability.

**Rank 2 (Level 3):**
- Increase your **Elemental [[2. Core Rules#Innate Defense|Innate Defense]]** by 1.
- When you would deal **Spell Damage**, you may **expend 1 additional energy** to manipluate the spell you are about to cast: Change the damage type of the ability to be one of the following: **Physical, Arcane, Electric, Fire, Freezing, or Poison damage**.
- Reduce the energy cost of abilities that deal **Spell Damage** by 1 (to a minimum of 1).

**Rank 3 (Level 10):** 
- When you would deal **Spell Damage**, you may **expend 1 additional energy** to manipluate the spell you are about to cast: Damage from this ability ignores **[[2. Core Rules#Innate Defense|Innate Defense]] and [[2. Core Rules#Damage Modifiers|Damage Resistance]]**.
- When you would deal **Spell Damage**, you may **expend 1 additional energy** to manipluate the spell you are about to cast: Increase the [[2. Core Rules#Dice Sizes|dice size]] of the damage roll(s) by 1 (maximum of d12).
