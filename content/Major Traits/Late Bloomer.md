You’re nothing special… For now.

**Rank 1:**

- All of your stats may be increased to a maximum of +7 instead of +6.

**Rank 2 (Level 6):**

- Choose two different stats to be increased by +2 each, up to a maximum of +7.
- Choose two weapon masteries to become rank 3.
- Choose another [[5. Major Traits|major trait]] (other than Late Bloomer). You gain its rank 1 benefits.

**Rank 3 (Level 12):**

- You now have **[[3. Character Creation#Prowess|prowess level]] 7 and a d20 [[3. Character Creation#Prowess|prowess die]]**. Any weapon mastery at rank 5 benefits from your prowess in that you use a d20 for the damage roll instead of a d12.
- When rolling [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|disfavor]], the roll becomes 1d10 instead of your **[[3. Character Creation#Prowess|prowess die]]**.
- Your chosen secondary [[5. Major Traits|major trait]] becomes rank 2.
