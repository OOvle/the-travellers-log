You are remarkable at melee combat. Your prowess with weaponry is above most. When you are on the battlefield, your enemies tremble as you are a force to reckon with.

**Rank 1:**

- Your blunt, slash, stab, ranged, and unarmed [[2. Core Rules#Weapon Mastery|weapon mastery]] ranks are now 2.
- Whenever a character leaves your melee range, you may perform an attack roll with your equipped melee weapon as a reaction.

**Rank** **2** **(Level 5):**

- When performing a weapon attack, add your **[[3. Character Creation#Prowess|prowess level]]** to the damage total.
- Your blunt, slash, stab, ranged, and unarmed [[2. Core Rules#Weapon Mastery|weapon mastery]] ranks that are not already 3 becomes rank 3.

**Rank 3 (level 10):**

- You cannot suffer [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|disfavor]] with weapon attacks.
- You may **expend 2 energy** during your turn to grant yourself 2 additional [[2. Core Rules#Actions|actions]] for your current turn. You may not do this more than once per [[2. Core Rules#Encounters|round]].
