Your defense is unbreakable. When you take a stand, you are an immovable wall that your opponents simply cannot move past. If anyone if going to break the line of defense, they must get through you first.

**Rank 1:**

- Your blunt [[2. Core Rules#Weapon Mastery|weapon mastery]] becomes 2.
- If you take the [[2. Core Rules#Actions|defend action]], you automatically win contests to resist being **wrestled**.
- While you’re defending, you may choose to redirect an attack to yourself if you are within range of the attack being performed.

**Rank 2 (Level 3):**

- Increase your block [[2. Core Rules#Dice Sizes|dice size]] by 1.
- If you take the [[2. Core Rules#Actions|defend action]], roll **1pd. All friendly characters within 6 yuels of yourself (including yourself)** add your roll to their block rolls until the end of this round.

**Rank 3 (Level 10):**

- Increase your block [[2. Core Rules#Dice Sizes|dice size]] by 1.
- You cannot suffer [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|disfavor]] with block rolls
- You can block attacks that normally cannot be blocked by **expending 1 energy** as a reaction.
