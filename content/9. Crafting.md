Some will want to engage with crafting. Crafting is simplified to only needing the dosh value in materials or plain dosh. Though this means that you can use iron to craft a wooden item, realism is not the focus of this system. However, if your party would prefer realism, your GM can rule specifications of the material type that you need to use in order to craft various objects, items, and so on.

# Standard Crafting

Standard crafting requires that you have materials or dosh that gets consumed to craft an item that you desire. There are tools for crafting weapons, armor, consumables, and construction respectively.

If you want to craft weapons, you will need [[Weapon Tools]]. Similarly if you want to craft any of the other types, you will need [[Armor Tools]], [[Consumable Tools]], or [[Constuction Tools]].

To begin crafting, you will need either a matching amount of dosh or materials worth the minimum value of the item you desire to craft. Next, you will need the associated tool to craft the item in question. Finally, you will need time. The time to craft an item is based on its value in dosh

# Advanced Crafting

The crafting tools do not change, however: A material requirement gets added to the usual dosh cost. Most weapons will require some form of metal or wood. Most armor will require fabrics, leather, or metals. If you want to craft consumables, you will need creature parts, herbs, and similar. And finally, if you want to build structures such as housing, fortifications, and similar, you will need wood, metal, stone, etc..

> Note: This is an optional rule and only required if the group agrees that they want their crafting to be a little more believable.
