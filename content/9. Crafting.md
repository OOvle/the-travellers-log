Crafting will be simplified to total material value, which is measured in dosh.

When crafting, you simply consume dosh and/or materials worth dosh equal to the item’s value and commit long rests to progress the craft. Crafting requires the ability associated with it. The time to craft an item is noted in the item's description.

Similar to crafting, enchantments may be done using dosh.

Some items may require a skill check to craft. A PC gets 1 roll per long rests in these cases. Unless specified by the GM, there is no punishment for failing this roll.
