You are mentally vulnerable to your charmer. You cannot attack your charmer willfully; you actively avoid actions that would cause direct harm to your charmer. Your charmer gains [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|favor]] to all rolls against you. If your charmer causes you harm, the charm wears off.
