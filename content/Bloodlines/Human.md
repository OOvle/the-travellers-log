Humans, you know them well. They populate nearly every mile of Jagadé Nimic. Humans are flexible and have a lot of variety among them; their skills vary a lot and each one specializes a little differently from the other.

As a Human, you can speak **Common (Donth)** and one other language of your choice.

1. **Proficient:** Increase one of your primary stats by +1. This bonus does not allow you to exceed the stat maximum of +6.
2. **Adaptive:** You may **expend 1 energy** to gain [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|favor]] on a [[2. Core Rules#Skill checks|skill check]].
