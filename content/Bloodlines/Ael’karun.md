A twisted mirror of the Ael’eth bloodline. Their appearance has been shaped by the very shadows into which they were cast. They retain the tall, slender frames and pointed ears of the Ael’eth, but every trace of starlight has been leached from them. Their skin is the color of cold ash, ranging from a deathly pale gray to a deep charcoal. Their hair, once silver and starlit, has darkened to ink black or the deep violet of a bruise, often worn in severe styles that accentuate their sharp features. Their eyes, once luminous and full of distant light, now burn with a cold, sullen ember, a smoldering crimson or a pale, icy violet, holding no warmth, only a simmering resentment. In complete darkness, a faint, unsettling glow emanates from their pupils, a predator's light.

As an Ael’karun, you can speak **Ethis (Alternate)** and **Common (Donth)**.

1. **Shifting Shadow:** When you are in total darkness, gain favor to dodge rolls.
2. **Shadow Touched:** When you roll to deal damage with a weapon, you may **expend 1 energy** to increase the damage by **1pd as Dark damage**.
