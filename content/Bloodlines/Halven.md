The Halven are a small and humble folk, shaped from the good soil of the meadow and the warmth of the hearth stone. They stand between three and four feet tall, with slightly pointed ears, rosy cheeks, and curly hair in shades of brown and auburn. Their feet are broad and covered with thick, downy hair, and they seldom wear shoes, preferring the feel of earth and grass beneath their soles. Though round and soft in appearance, they are deceptively hardy, with a stamina that allows them to walk long distances without complaint.

As a Halven, you can speak **Common (Donth)** and one other language of your choice.

1. **Jolly:** Once per [[2. Core Rules#Resting|long rest]], you may spread joyous laughter. When you do this, roll **1pd**. **All characters within 6 yuels of yourself** regains energy equal to what you rolled.
2. **Carefree:** You are immune to being **[[Terrified]]**. When rolling to resist being **[[Charmed]]**, gain [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|favor]].
