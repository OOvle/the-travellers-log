### Abilities

**Ancient Spirt**: Passive
Normal, Elemental, and Special [[2. Core Rules#Innate Defense|innate defenses]] are increased by 5 each.

**Jaded Spirit**: Passive
This character is immune to being [[Blinded]], [[Charmed]], [[Terrified]], [[Petrified]], and [[Taunted]].

**Intense Presence**: Passive
Whenever another character moves within 6 yuels of yourself or starts their turn there, that character becomes [[Slowed]] and suffers **1d12 mental damage ([[2. Core Rules#Damage Properties|surefire]])** until the end of their next turn.

**Unstoppable Force**: Passive / 4 Energy
Once per round, you may **expend 4 energy** to grant yourself 4 additional actions for this turn.

**Absurd Resilience**: Reaction / 2 Energy
When you must make a resistance roll, automatically pass it instead.

**Spirit Sight**: Passive
This character has [[11. Game Master's Tools#True Sight|true sight]] up to 120 yuels.

**Cry Of The Ancient**: 2 Actions / 3 Energy / 240 yuel radius
All other characters within range suffers **2d10+5 soul damage ([[2. Core Rules#Damage Properties|unavoidable]]) and 2d10+5 booming damage ([[2. Core Rules#Damage Properties|unavoidable]])**. Each character within range must also make a WLL resistance roll. On a failed roll, they become [[Shaken]] for the next 1 minute.

**Mighty Slam**: 2 Actions / 0 Energy / Melee (3 yuels); 12 yuel radius
Choose a character within range. The target suffers **2d10+2 physical damage and 2d10+2 soul damage**. All other characters within 12 yuels of the original taget suffers **1d10 booming damage ([[2. Core Rules#Damage Properties|unblockable]])**.

**Shape Terrain**: 2 Actions / 10 Energy / 240 yuel radius
Drastically change the landscape within range however you desire. You may make the land become mountainous, create a chasm, create a wall of earth, and so on.

**Extreme Gale**: 2 Actions / 3 Energy / 60 yuel radius
All other characters within range must make a **MGT resistance roll (DR 17)**. Each character who fails this roll is pushed up to 20 yuels towards or away from you (chosen upon use). Characters will always stop within 1 yuel of yourself if they are less than 20 yuels away from you when pulled.

**Forest Roots**: 2 Actions / 3 Energy / 120 yuel radius
All other characters that you choose must make an **AGI resistance roll (DR 15)**. On a failed roll, they become [[Rooted]] for the next 10 minutes. Characters rooted this way may break free early by using 2 actions to make a **MGT resistance roll (DR 16)**.
