### Abilities

**Bite**: 2 Actions / 0 Energy / Melee (1 yuel)

Deal 1d6+1 physical damage. If this attack isn't dodged, make a wrestle contest against your target. If the target loses the contest, they become grappled.

**Bear Revenge**: 1 Action / 1 Energy

Each time this character is targeted for this round, it may move up to half its speed towards the attacker regardless of the outcome of the interaction. If this character reaches its attacker with the movement, it may perform a claw attack against its attacker.

**Claw**: 1 Action / 0 Energy / Melee (1 yuel)

Deal 1d4+1 physical damage.

**Maul**: Reaction / 1 Energy

When this character makes a bite attack, it becomes empowered. If the target of this attack does not successfully dodge, **they must make an END resistance roll (DR 6)**. On a failed roll, the target becomes [[Slowed]] until the end of their next turn.
