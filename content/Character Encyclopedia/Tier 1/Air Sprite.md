#### [[10. Character Encyclopedia#Categories|Spirit]], Soldier

A small puff of sentient cloud. Usually harmless, but can become dangerous quickly if it gets charged with electricity.

| Stat | Value | Stat       | Value      |
| ---- | ----- | ---------- | ---------- |
| AGI  | 3     | Max Health | 12 (2d4+7) |
| MGT  | -2    | Max Energy | 6          |
| END  | 1     | Move Speed | 8          |
| WLL  | 1     | Block Roll | None       |
| WIS  | -1    | Dodge Roll | 1d10+1     |
| Size | Small | Prowess    | 1          |

### Abilities

**Zap**: 2 Actions ![[RPG Icons/Warrior/Warrior Skills (1).png|24]] / Up to 3 Energy ![[RPG Icons/Elemental/Lightning/Lightning Magic (7).png|24]] / 12 yuels ![[RPG Icons/Archer/Archer Skills (30).png|24]]

Deal 1d4 electric damage. If Air Sprite has any energy, consume up to 3 energy to increase the damage roll by a number of d4s equal to the additional energy spent. Dodge rolls suffer [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|disfavor]] against this attack.

**Conductive**: Passive ![[RPG Icons/Other/Other (208).png|24]]

Air Sprite is immune to electric damage. Whenever Air Sprite would suffer electric damage, it gains energy equal to half of the damage it would have normally suffered.

At the end of each round, Air Sprite restores 1 missing energy. This is increased to 2 energy if another Air Sprite is within 2 yuels of itself.

**Weightless**: Passive ![[RPG Icons/Other/Other (208).png|24]]

Air Sprite always fails resistance rolls that would cause it to move against its will.

**Cumulonimbus**: Passive ![[RPG Icons/Other/Other (208).png|24]]

Air Sprite's current energy at the start of combat reflects the weather around it.

- If the weather is raining, Air Sprite has 2 energy at the start of combat.
- If the weather is storming, Air Sprite has 3 energy at the start of combat.
- Any other weather type, Air Sprite has no energy at the start of combat.

**Discharge**: 2 Actions ![[RPG Icons/Warrior/Warrior Skills (1).png|24]] / 6 Energy ![[RPG Icons/Elemental/Lightning/Lightning Magic (7).png|24]] / 6 yuels ![[RPG Icons/Archer/Archer Skills (30).png|24]]

All characters within range suffers 1d12+6 electric damage ([[2. Core Rules#Damage Properties|unavoidable]]). Each character that suffers damage from this attack must make an endurance resistance roll (DR 8). Each character that fails this roll becomes [[Dazed]] until the end of the next round.
