Hostile characters are measured by tiers.

- Tier 1: level 1-2
- Tier 2: level 3-5
- Tier 3: level 6-8
- Tier 4: level 9-11
- Tier 5: level 12
- Special: anything above tier 5 goes into this tier.

Within these tiers, hostiles are categorized by…

- Grunts (easy to kill enemies, often in groups; low health, low damage, basic attack pattern; common)
- Soldiers (combatant, variation of attack; low to moderate health, low to moderate damage, basic attack pattern; somewhat common)
- Commanders (mini boss, tough enemy with moderate variations of attack; moderate to high health, moderate to high damage; uncommon)
- Generals (boss, solo or has grunts; high health, high damage, unique gimmicks and attack patterns; rare/payoff/only one for the session)

Enemy designs should all be interesting when isolated. The only exception is grunt enemies - They are, by design, to be fodder and simple; maximum gimmicks they are allowed is one simple gimmick.

|  Large   | 2^2  | 4 spaces  |
|   Huge   | 3^2  | 9 spaces  |
| Massive  | 4^2  | 16 spaces |
| Colossal | 5^2  | 25 spaces |

## Categories

There are the following categories for characters: **Abomination, Beast, Floral, Humanoid, Insect, Nightmare, Puppet, Reverent, Spirit, Undead, and Behemoth**.
