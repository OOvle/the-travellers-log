# Vision

Characters will have various senses for seeing such as "tremor sense," "magic vision," and so on. Below is the list of each vision and the benefits they provide.

## Normal Vision

Most characters have normal vision; seeing with the naked eye. This has no unique advantages.

## Dark Vision

You can see in dim light as if it were bright light and you can see in darkness as if it were dim light. When you are seeing in total darkness, you cannot make out colors, only shades of black and white.

## Magic Vision

You can see magic for what it is. Anytime you see magic before you, you can immediately recognize that it is so. You automatically pass WIS rolls to detect illusions, but you cannot see through illusions and similar. Magic vision does not allow you to see in the dark.

## Tremor Sense

You can feel the vibrations of the ground beneath you. You can tell the direction and size of any character wtihin range of your tremor sense. You cannot detect if the character is hostile or what it is by this feeling alone, only where it is and where it's moving.

## Blind Sight

You can see within range without needing to physically look. You cannot see any colors, only that there is objects, walls, characters, and similar within your blind sight.

## True Sight

You can see through illusions as if they were a transparent projection, the real form of characters who are polymorphed into a different creature, in total darkness and dim light as if it were bright light, and you can make out colors in complete darkness.
