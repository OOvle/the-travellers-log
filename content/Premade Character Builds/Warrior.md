# Notes

The goal of the warrior is to be a powerful combatant in the front lines. This balances between increasing your max health and your **MGT**. By upping your max health and **MGT**, you will have a solid amount of health to work with as well as consistent damage. Equipment you want to aim for is heavy armors and slash weapons.
The [[Fighter]] major trait makes it possible to use any physical weapon effectively without losing too much damage potential. [[Pummel]] is a powerful utility that gives you the opportunity to stop enemies from doing powerful attacks. [[Efficient Strikes]] is your bread and butter. Since you will primarily be using weapon attacks for your damage, it gives you more of them in each turn. It also leaves room for you to run potentially other actions during your turn.
By level 10, you will be a powerhouse hitting consistently high damage and have access to Execution, a remarkable damage ability that allows you to hit really hard against characters who are [[2. Core Rules#Wounded Threshold|wounded]].
