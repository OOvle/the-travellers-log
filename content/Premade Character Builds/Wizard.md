# Notes

[[Shield Mastery]] and [[Shield Bash]] are going to be your primary tools for this set up. The entire build is focused on being an immovable wall. While you are in the front guarding attacks, you will be using your [[Shield Bash]] to disable the enemy’s guard and [[Taunting Strike]] to get their focus on you. You will gradually increase your **MGT** as you level up to improve your shield’s value as well as improve your ability to land abilities such as [[Taunting Strike]].
When you unlock [[Unrelenting Guard]] and [[Efficient Strikes]], you become even harder to kill, while also getting the opportunity to dish out some more damage against your enemies, instead of going mostly on one or the other.
Finally, by level 10, you unlock your major trait’s best feature: The ability to block attacks that ignore block! Additionally, we acquire [[Heavy Strike]], which allows you to reposition enemies away from your allies.

By level 12, your stats should look something like so:
