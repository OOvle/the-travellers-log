Your bloodline represents your lineage or ancestors. Whether you hail from the ancient Ael’eth, lived deep in the mountains as a stout Duroka, or a gleeful Halven from the glade. Each one determines your general appearance and some roleplay aspects of your character. Each bloodline has **two unique traits** that you gain when creating your character.

# Mixed Bloodline

If none of the bloodlines appeal to you, you may discuss with your GM to make a mixed bloodline which mixes two bloodlines together (shocking, I know). When creating a custom bloodline, you must choose the first trait from one bloodline and the second from another. If you choose the first trait of the Human bloodline, you may not choose the first trait of any other bloodline; you must choose the second trait of another bloodline. You cannot mix more than 2 bloodlines mechanically, but if you want a 3rd, or even 4th bloodline mixed into your character, the additional bloodlines must be purely cosmetic and nothing more.

# [[Human]]

Humans, you know them well. They populate nearly every mile of Jagadé Nimic. Humans are flexible and have a lot of variety among them; their skills vary a lot and each one specializes a little differently from the other.

As a Human, you can speak **Common (Donth)** and one other language of your choice.

1. **Proficient:** Increase one of your primary stats by +1. This bonus does not allow you to exceed the stat maximum of +6.
2. **Adaptive:** You may **expend 1 energy** to gain [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|favor]] on a [[2. Core Rules#Skill checks|skill check]].

# [[Ael’eth]]

The Ael’eth are a tall and graceful people, their forms woven from starlight and the morning dew. They stand taller than humans, with slender frames that move with an effortless, fluid elegance. Their skin carries a faint luminescence in low light, as though a sliver of the night sky rests beneath the surface of their skin. Hair ranges from spun silver to deep indigo, often worn long and unadorned. Their eyes are their most striking feature, large and luminous, reflecting the light of distant stars even in the darkest chambers. Their ears taper to a delicate point, sensitive to the faintest shifts in sound. The Ael’eth age slowly, living many centuries without visible decay, which grants them a perspective of patient detachment that shorter lived races can find unsettling.

As an Ael’eth, you can speak **Ethis** and **Common (Donth)**.

1. **Flow Like a River:** Increase your max energy by 2.
2. **One With The Stars:** Increase your **Special [[2. Core Rules#Innate Defense|Innate Defense]]** by 1.

# [[Ael’karun]]

A twisted mirror of the Ael’eth bloodline. Their appearance has been shaped by the very shadows into which they were cast. They retain the tall, slender frames and pointed ears of the Ael’eth, but every trace of starlight has been leached from them. Their skin is the color of cold ash, ranging from a deathly pale gray to a deep charcoal. Their hair, once silver and starlit, has darkened to ink black or the deep violet of a bruise, often worn in severe styles that accentuate their sharp features. Their eyes, once luminous and full of distant light, now burn with a cold, sullen ember, a smoldering crimson or a pale, icy violet, holding no warmth, only a simmering resentment. In complete darkness, a faint, unsettling glow emanates from their pupils, a predator's light.

As an Ael’karun, you can speak **Ethis (Alternate)** and **Common (Donth)**.

1. **Shifting Shadow:** While you are in total darkness, gain [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|favor]] to dodge rolls.
2. **Shadow Touched:** When you roll to deal damage with a weapon, you may **expend 1 energy** to increase the damage by **1pd as Dark damage**.

# [[Duroka]]

The Duroka are a stocky, powerful people carved from the heartstone of the world's deepest mountains. They stand shorter than humans, rarely exceeding four and a half feet, but their bodies are dense with muscle and built for endurance. Their skin tones range from pale granite to rich umber, often textured like fine stone. Hair is thick and coarse, colored in earthen shades of black, brown, rust red, and gray, and both males and females wear it long, often braided with metal bands. Their hands are broad and calloused, their fingers thick and deft. The Duroka possess a natural resilience to heat, cold, and the crushing pressures of the deep places.

As a Duroka, you can speak **Grók** and **Common (Donth)**.

1. **Stout Sturdiness:** Increase your block [[2. Core Rules#Dice Sizes|dice size]] by 1.
2. **Heart Of The Mountain:** When you make an **END** resistance roll, gain [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|favor]].

# [[Halven]]

The Halven are a small and humble folk, shaped from the good soil of the meadow and the warmth of the hearth stone. They stand between three and four feet tall, with slightly pointed ears, rosy cheeks, and curly hair in shades of brown and auburn. Their feet are broad and covered with thick, downy hair, and they seldom wear shoes, preferring the feel of earth and grass beneath their soles. Though round and soft in appearance, they are deceptively hardy, with a stamina that allows them to walk long distances without complaint.

As a Halven, you can speak **Common (Donth)** and one other language of your choice.

1. **Jolly:** Once per [[2. Core Rules#Resting|long rest]], you may spread joyous laughter. When you do this, roll **1pd**. **All characters within 6 yuels of yourself** regains energy equal to what you rolled.
2. **Carefree:** You are immune to being **[[Terrified]]**. When rolling to resist being **[[Charmed]]**, gain [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|favor]].

# [[Beastkin]]

The Beastkin are a people of many forms, humanoid in frame but bearing the features of beast and bird. Their appearance varies by strain. All Beastkin share a common difficulty with labial sounds due to their non-human mouths, a trait that shapes their language, Loxiq, and their names.

As a Beastkin, you can speak **Loxiq** and **Common (Donth; Simple)**.

## [[Beastkin#Kanidar|Kanidar]]

The Kanidar (Canine) possess wolfish muzzles, pointed ears atop their heads, furred bodies, and somewhat bushy tails.

1. **Hunter:** When rolling to track or search for another character, gain [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|favor]].
2. **Pack Tactics:** As long as you are within 1 yuel of an ally, gain [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|favor]] to all weapon damage rolls against your target.

## [[Beastkin#Helithar|Helithar]]

The Helithar (Feline) are sleek and lithe, with slit pupiled eyes, whiskers, retractable claws, and coats ranging from tawny gold to midnight black.

1. **Nine Lives:** You have **[[2. Core Rules#Damage Modifiers|resistance]]** to fall damage.
2. **Claws:** When rolling for **unarmed damage**, increase the damage by **1pd physical damage**.

## [[Beastkin#Adesar|Adesar]]

The Adesar (Avian) are covered in feathers rather than fur, with beaked faces or partial beaks set upon humanoid features, light hollow bones, and wings that may be broad and functional or cruelly pinioned from their time in captivity.

1. **Wings:** You gain a flying speed equal to your move speed.
2. **Sharp Eyes:** When rolling to spot, look, or investigate, gain [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|favor]].

## [[Beastkin#Kulasar|Kulasar]]

Similar to the Kanidar, Kulasar (Vulpine) possess fox-like muzzles, pointed ears atop their heads, furred bodies, and somewhat bushy tails.

1. **Clever Fox:** Increase your dodge [[2. Core Rules#Dice Sizes|dice size]] by 1.
2. **Scavenger:** When searching or looting, add your **[[3. Character Creation#Prowess|prowess dice]]** to the roll.

## [[Gorék]]

The Gorék are hulking brutes, their bodies a chaotic canvas of the Artificers' failed ambition. They stand taller than most humans, often exceeding six and a half feet, with massively muscled frames that rival the Duroka in breadth. Their skin is their most unsettling feature, a mottled blend of sickly green and deep crimson red that shifts in patches across their bodies. Their teeth are garish and jagged, too large for their mouths, jutting at angles that make their faces a mask of perpetual ferocity. Their hands are enormous, capable of crushing stone, and their voices are guttural roars that carry for miles across the wastelands.

As a Gorék, you can speak **Kalehk** and **Common (Donth; Simple)**.

1. **ME MIGHTY:** When rolling to wrestle or resist being wrestled, gain [[2. Core Rules#Favor & Disfavor|favor]].
2. **ME STRONK:** When rolling to deal damage with a melee weapon attack, increase it by **1d6 physical damage**.
